# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be direct, technically honest, and respectful. Review claims against evidence, criticize the work rather than the person, and make room for maintainers and contributors with different levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing, credential exposure, and publication of private deployment data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile. The maintainer may edit, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate this standard.
